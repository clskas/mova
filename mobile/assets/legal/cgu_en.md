## 2. Services offered

SENGA connects passenger users with independent drivers and couriers for:

- **Taxi / Motorbike taxi** — immediate rides in town
- **Parcel delivery** — sending and tracking parcels
- **Scheduled booking** — rides planned up to 7 days ahead
- **Food delivery** — orders from partner restaurants
- **Shopping & errands** — purchases made by a courier on the passenger's behalf
- **Carpooling** — sharing trips between users
- **Vehicle rental** — renting a car, SUV or minibus
- **Express delivery** — urgent delivery of small parcels
- **Moving** — transport and handling with a truck
- **SENGA wallet** — balance, top-up and payments
- **History** — viewing past transactions

SENGA acts solely as a technology intermediary. Transport and delivery providers are independent workers.
