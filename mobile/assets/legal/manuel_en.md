# Senga

**Version:** 1.1 — July 2026  
**Market:** Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa)

## Welcome to SENGA

SENGA is your urban mobility app in the DRC. Order a taxi or motorbike taxi (including a **Pool shared ride**), send a parcel, book a trip in advance, order a meal, do your shopping or share a **scheduled carpool** — all in Congolese francs (CDF).

## 1. Create an account

1. Open the Senga app.
2. **Animated home screen** (**4 s per service**, ~32 s in total): an overview of the services (taxi, deliveries, wallet, etc.). Tap the screen to go straight to sign-in.
3. Enter your phone number in the **+243** format followed by 9 digits.
4. Enter the OTP code received by SMS.
5. You reach the home screen: the **city** shown matches your GPS position (you can change it with the selector at the top).

## 1.1 Home screen — order of the services

The services are shown in a **2×4 grid** (top to bottom, left then right):

| Row | Left | Right |
|-----|------|-------|
| 1 | Taxi / Moto-taxi | Deliveries |
| 2 | Scheduled booking | Carpool |
| 3 | Vehicle rental | Moving |
| 4 | SENGA Wallet | History |

**SENGA Wallet** and **History** are at the bottom of the grid. You can also reach them from the navigation bar (wallet and clock icons) and the menu at the top of the screen.

## 2. Taxi / Moto-taxi

1. Tap **Taxi / Moto-taxi**.
2. Enter your destination (e.g. Gombe, Limete, Masina).
3. Choose the vehicle type: Moto-taxi, Standard, Comfort, VIP, **Family** (6 to 8 seats) or **Tricycle** (passenger tricycle, 3 seats maximum, cheaper than a car — when available in your city).
   - By car, set the **number of passengers** with the − and + buttons. Standard, Comfort and VIP: **4 passengers maximum**.
   - From **5 passengers**, the app switches to **Family** automatically. The price is per vehicle, not per passenger.
   - Only cars with enough seats receive your request: with 7 or 8 passengers, the wait may be longer.
4. Tap **Estimate the price**, then **Confirm the ride**.
5. Follow your driver in real time on the tracking screen.
6. A **blue line** on the map shows the route already travelled (GPS trace), in addition to the driver's current position.
7. Under the map, use the **POI filters** (Markets, Hospitals, Universities, Pharmacies) to show nearby points of interest.
8. Destination autocomplete includes **SENGA places** (markets, hospitals, etc.) as well as districts.

## 2.1 Shared ride (Pool)

On **Taxi** (car only, not motorbike), turn on the **Shared (Pool)** toggle before confirming.

1. Estimate the price: the Pool fare is **lower** than an exclusive ride (around −35%).
2. Confirm: SENGA looks for a vehicle that can take several passengers on compatible routes.
3. You can **join** a ride already in progress if a seat is free — tracking opens directly if a driver is already assigned.
4. After your drop-off, pay for **your seat** (wallet, Mobile Money or cash) — not the total for all passengers.
5. Detours are possible to pick up / drop off other passengers.

> **Difference from Carpool**: Pool is an **on-demand** taxi ride. **Carpool** (its own menu) is a **scheduled** trip, BlaBlaCar style (seats on a trip published in advance).

## 3. Parcel delivery

1. Tap **Parcel delivery**.
2. Enter the pickup and delivery addresses.
3. Select the weight category (documents, small, medium, large).
4. Add a photo (optional).
5. Estimate, confirm, then follow your parcel.
6. On the tracking screen, the **GPS trace** (line on the map) shows the courier's route.

## 4. Scheduled booking

1. Tap **Scheduled booking**.
2. See your upcoming bookings at the top of the screen.
3. Choose the date and time (up to 7 days ahead).
4. Enter the destination and the vehicle you want.
5. Confirm the booking.
6. You will get **reminders** the day before and one hour before departure (in-app notification, no operator SMS).
7. A driver is assigned automatically about **2 hours** before the scheduled time (or manually by the SENGA team).
8. **Late cancellation** (less than 24 h before departure, driver already confirmed): a fee of **50%** of the estimated fare may be taken from your wallet.

## 5. Shop delivery (meals & stores)

1. Tap **Deliveries**, then **Shop delivery** (or the meals entry on the home screen).
2. Filter by type: **All**, **Restaurants**, **Supermarkets**, **Pharmacies**, **Shops**.
3. Choose a store from the list (a type badge is shown on each card).
4. Browse the **menu** (restaurant) or the **catalogue** (other types) and add items to the cart.
5. Depending on the store: choose a **size** / **options**; confirm your age or the **prescription** if asked; out-of-stock items cannot be added.
6. Enter the delivery address and order.
7. Follow the delivery in real time (position + route trace).

## 6. Shopping & errands

1. Tap **Shopping & errands**.
2. Enter the **pickup point** (shop, pharmacy, market) — use autocomplete to pick a known place.
3. List the items to buy.
4. Enter the delivery address (or **My location**).
5. Optional: set a **maximum shopping budget** — the amount is **held** on your SENGA wallet until the errand ends.
6. Estimate the price (service fee + estimated purchases depending on the type of shop).
7. Send the list: a nearby courier receives an **alert**.
8. Once the courier is assigned, use the **chat** to clarify an unavailable item.
9. At the end, pay the service fee and the actual amount of the purchases.

## 7. Carpool (scheduled)

1. Tap **Carpool**.
2. **Search**: enter the departure city, the destination and the date. Sort by price, time or driver rating. Each result shows the distance, the ETA and the KYC badge if the driver is verified.
3. **Book**: select a trip, choose the number of seats and confirm. You see the driver summary and can contact the driver (masked phone number).
4. **My bookings**: see your booked trips. Cancel before departure if needed.
5. **Trip details**: route map, timeline (Published → Seats booked → On the way → Completed).

> Publishing trips is reserved for **SENGA drivers** (Driver app, approved KYC). This is **not** taxi Pool (section 2.1).

## 8. Express delivery

1. Tap **Express delivery**.
2. Enter the pickup and delivery for an urgent shipment (under 45 min).
3. Estimate the fare, confirm and follow the courier in real time.

## 9. Vehicle rental

1. Tap **Vehicle rental**.
2. Choose the type (car, SUV, minibus) and the duration. On the vehicle page, **swipe the photos** (up to 5) to see it from every angle.
3. Enter the dates and the pickup place.
4. Confirm the booking.

## 10. Moving

1. Tap **Moving**.
2. Choose the **home type** (studio, apartment, house, office) and the number of rooms: the app estimates the volume in m³.
3. The app suggests the **suitable vehicle** (van, 15, 30 or 50 m³ truck); you can change it.
4. Enter the departure and arrival addresses, the **list of items** and **photos** (optional) to help the driver.
5. Check the price (trip + fixed fees + volume × vehicle) and confirm.
6. Drivers with a large enough **van or truck** are notified; the **first one to accept** takes your move. You are notified as soon as a driver is assigned.
7. You can **cancel** as long as the move has not started. At the end, pay in the app or in cash.

## 11. SENGA Wallet

Card on the **last row** of the home screen (or wallet icon in the bottom bar). See your **withdrawable balance**, top up via M-Pesa or Airtel Money (Orange Money depending on availability), and pay for your rides from the wallet.

- **Top-up**: minimum **2,300 FC**; confirm the operator prompt on the SIM's phone.
- **Withdrawal** to Mobile Money: same minimum **2,300 FC**, SMS OTP code required. Only the **withdrawable balance** (wallet − held funds) can be withdrawn.
- **Cash** payments for a ride do **not** credit your wallet (nor the driver's as a withdrawable amount). Hand the money to the driver; they confirm **Cash received** (no PIN code to share for the payment).
- **After a cash ride**, you can **order a new ride** even if the driver has not confirmed yet. The payment stays pending until **Cash received**, your **"I paid in cash"** declaration, or an **automatic closing within 24 h**.
- The **I paid in cash** button is on the cash payment screen, on the **tracking** screen while the payment is pending, and in the **dispute** window if the driver or courier tapped **Cash not received**. This declaration does not clear the dispute: the driver still has to confirm **I received the cash**, or you pay another way, or support closes the case.
- If the driver reports **Cash not received**, a **dispute** opens: you cannot order again until it is resolved (driver confirmation, another payment, or SENGA support).
- Ride status changes (accepted, driver arrived, in progress, completed) and scheduled ride reminders arrive as **app notifications**. No operator SMS is sent for these messages.

## 12. History

Card on the **last row** of the home screen (or clock icon in the bottom bar). Tabs: **Rides** (taxi, shopping & errands, moves) | **Parcels** | **Meals** | **Bookings** (scheduled rides, **rentals** and **carpools**).

- Each line shows its **date and time**.
- Choose a **month** at the top: the number of orders and the **total spent** show for the open tab.
- **Load more** shows older orders; the ↻ icon refreshes the list.
- Tap a line for the details, the receipt, or **Report a problem** (lost item, behaviour, vehicle, price, accident…). The SENGA team gets back to you; for an emergency, use the **SOS** button.

## 13. SOS alert and emergency contacts

- During a ride, the red **SOS** button alerts the SENGA team with your position. **Tap it**: a confirmation window opens. **Hold it for 3 seconds**: the alert is sent at once.
- After sending, a window shows where the alert stands: **Waiting for an agent**, then **Taken in charge** (agent's name), then **Closed**. Your position is shared with the team for **30 minutes**; you can stop this sharing.
- **Without Internet**, the app offers to **send the alert by SMS** to the SENGA duty number, or to **call emergency services** (112, police…).
- **Emergency contacts** (Profile): save up to **3 people**. If you raise a SOS, they get an SMS with your position.

## 14. Real-time GPS tracking

During an active **ride**, **parcel delivery**, **meal**, **express** or **shopping & errand**:

| Map element | Meaning |
|-------------|---------|
| Driver / courier marker | Current position |
| Blue line | Route already travelled (updated automatically) |
| Start / end points | Order addresses |

The trace builds up as the trip goes on. With a weak network, the position may lag; the trace fills in when the connection is back.

## 16. App updates

When a new version is available for your phone, an **"Update" banner** appears at the top of the app. Tap it to install the update from the Play Store or the App Store. New versions are sometimes rolled out gradually: it is normal for someone close to you to get it before you.
