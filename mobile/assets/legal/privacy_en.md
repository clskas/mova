# Privacy Policy — SENGA DRC

> **Translation for information only.** This English version is provided for convenience. The French version of this Privacy Policy is the only legally binding version; in case of any difference, the French version prevails.

**Last updated:** 12 June 2026  
**Data controller:** SENGA SARL, Kinshasa, DRC  
**DPO:** privacy@mova.cd

## 1. Introduction

SENGA is committed to protecting the privacy of the users of its mobile application (Passenger and Driver) in accordance with the Congolese legislation applicable to personal data protection.

This policy explains what data we collect, why, for how long, and what your rights are.

## 2. Data collected

### 2.1 Identification data
- Phone number (+243)
- Name (optional)
- Profile photo (optional)

### 2.2 Location data
- Real-time GPS position **during** active rides, deliveries and trips
- After a SOS alert, your position is shared with the SENGA team for 30 minutes (you can stop this sharing)
- Departure, arrival and delivery addresses you enter

### 2.3 Transaction data
- History of rides, parcels, meals, bookings and errands
- Amounts in CDF, payment methods used
- SENGA wallet balance

### 2.4 Driver data (KYC)
- Driving licence
- Vehicle registration card
- ID photo
- Availability status and earnings

### 2.5 Emergency contacts (optional)
- Name and phone number of up to 3 people, entered by you in your Profile
- Used only to send them an SMS with your position when you raise a SOS alert
- Deleted when you remove them or when your account is deleted. Let these people know before adding them.

### 2.6 Technical data
- Device identifier
- Application version
- Anonymised error logs

## 3. Purposes of processing

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---------|-------------|
| Providing mobility and delivery services | Performance of the contract |
| Processing mobile money payments | Performance of the contract |
| Keeping users safe | Legitimate interest |
| Verifying drivers' identity (KYC) | Legal obligation |
| Improving the application | Legitimate interest |
| Customer support | Performance of the contract |

## 4. Data sharing

SENGA **does not sell** your data. It may be shared with:

- **Payment providers**: Orange Money, M-Pesa, Airtel Money (processing transactions)
- **Drivers/couriers**: information needed for the service (name, masked phone number, addresses)
- **Competent authorities**: on legal request or in an emergency
- **Technical providers**: cloud hosting, under a confidentiality agreement

## 5. Retention

| Type of data | Duration |
|--------------|----------|
| Active account | Duration of the contractual relationship |
| Ride history | 3 years after the last transaction |
| Driver KYC documents | 5 years after the end of the collaboration |
| Technical logs | 12 months |

## 6. Security

SENGA applies technical and organisational measures: TLS encryption of communications, JWT authentication, restricted access to data, regular backups.

## 7. Your rights

Under Congolese legislation, you have the following rights:

- **Access** — get a copy of your data
- **Rectification** — correct inaccurate data
- **Erasure** — ask for your account to be deleted
- **Objection** — object to certain processing
- **Portability** — receive your data in a structured format

To exercise your rights: **privacy@mova.cd** or WhatsApp +243 900 000 000.

## 8. Cookies and trackers

The mobile application does not use web cookies. Local identifiers (authentication token) are stored securely on your device.

## 9. Minors

SENGA is not intended for people under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

## 10. International transfers

Data is hosted primarily in Africa. Any transfer outside the DRC is subject to appropriate safeguards.

This policy may be updated. The date of the last revision is shown at the top of the document. Substantial changes will be notified in the application.
